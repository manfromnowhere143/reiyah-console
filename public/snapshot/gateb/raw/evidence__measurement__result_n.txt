FINDING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Across the entire operating range 0.1 to 0.5, for BOTH lidar detectors, the conditional
coefficient's 95% interval excludes independence. Ten measurements, ten intervals above
1.0. The residual camera-lidar coupling is not an artifact of the 0.3 operating point.

Two honest facts, stated as measured, not softened and not oversold:

  1. It attenuates. The conditional c declines as the threshold tightens (megvii
     1.360 -> 1.051; pointpillars 1.249 -> 1.043). As only confident detections remain,
     the residual coupling shrinks toward independence. At score >= 0.5 the effect is
     small - about 5% and 4% excess - though still statistically distinguishable from 1.0.

  2. It never reaches independence in the measured range. The lower bound of every
     interval at every threshold, for both detectors, is above 1.0. The marginal
     coefficient (shared difficulty plus coupling) falls faster and further; the part
     that survives conditioning falls slower and does not cross 1.0 anywhere measured.

Combined with Result M (replication across a second lidar backbone), the finding is now
robust along two independent axes: the detector, and the score threshold. The cheapest
single-operating-point objection to Results L and M is answered on evidence.

NON-CLAIMS
  Association after declared conditioning, not a causal effect. Bounded by the covariates
  nuScenes annotates (object size and truncation were not available and not tested).
  Unmeasured common causes remain possible; no adjustment set is claimed sufficient for
  identification. Two published detection outputs on one public split, retained as
  proposed. No released 1.2 byte is modified.
