========================================================================================
AUDIT OF THE RESULT H ACCURACY CLAIM
"joint-failure odds rise with the accuracy of both models: 7.01, 15.86, 31.99"
========================================================================================

### 1. provenance of the claim
  stated in : docs/GATE_B_SESSION_HANDOFF.md, docs/GATE_B_MEASUREMENT_CONTRACT.md
  labelled  : 'measured' in the contract results table
  computed  : nowhere. result_h.py binds MAP and never reads it.
  status    : a prose ordering of three published numbers

### 2. does 'accuracy of both models' even order the pairs?
  pair                            min mAP  sum mAP   cond c     MH OR
  centerpoint x megvii               51.9    113.5    1.725    31.988
  centerpoint x pointpillars         29.5     91.1    1.386    15.863
  megvii x pointpillars              29.5     81.4    1.313     7.010

  Two pairs share an identical weaker-model accuracy of 29.5 mAP:
    centerpoint x pointpillars     MH OR = 15.863
    megvii x pointpillars     MH OR = 7.010
  Their odds ratios differ by 2.26x at the same weaker-model
  accuracy. 'The accuracy of both models' therefore does not order
  these data unless 'both' silently means the sum, which is a
  different and unstated claim.

### 3. evidential weight of a monotone trend on three points
  permutations of three distinct values      : 6
  that are monotone increasing               : 1   p = 0.167
  that are monotone in either direction      : 2   p = 0.333
  Under a random assignment of the three odds ratios to the three
  accuracy ranks, a perfect monotone trend arises one time in six.
  No conventional threshold is met. There is no test statistic to
  report because there is no test.

### 4. the three points are not three independent observations
  detectors involved: centerpoint, megvii, pointpillars
    centerpoint    appears in 2 of the 3 pairs
    megvii         appears in 2 of the 3 pairs
    pointpillars   appears in 2 of the 3 pairs
  Every detector appears in two pairs, so the three odds ratios share
  detectors pairwise. They cannot be treated as independent draws.

### 5. one accuracy figure is not validated
    centerpoint     61.6 mAP   NOT VALIDATED  in 2 of 3 pairs
    megvii          51.9 mAP   validated      in 2 of 3 pairs
    pointpillars    29.5 mAP   validated      in 2 of 3 pairs
  2 of the 3 points depend on an accuracy figure the workstream
  itself records as reconstructed and unconfirmed, from a source it
  marks 'explicitly weaker provenance'.

### 6. the headline switches to the more dramatic metric
  Result G states that the lift c, not the odds ratio, 'is the number
  that belongs against Corollary 3'. The same three pairs in that metric:
    centerpoint x megvii            c = 1.725   MH OR = 31.988
    centerpoint x pointpillars      c = 1.386   MH OR = 15.863
    megvii x pointpillars           c = 1.313   MH OR = 7.010
  spread in c      : 1.31x
  spread in MH OR  : 4.56x
  The quoted figures are the metric with the widest spread, while the
  workstream's own analysis names the other one as operative.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Finding. The claim 'joint-failure odds rise with the accuracy of both
models' is not supported and must not carry the label 'measured'.

It is an ordering of three non-independent odds ratios, two of which
depend on an unvalidated accuracy figure, expressed in a metric the
workstream's own analysis does not treat as operative, with a
permutation p of 0.167 for the trend and no interval on any point.
Two pairs at identical weaker-model accuracy differ by 2.26x, so the
stated covariate does not even order the observations.

This does not touch Result H's supported finding. Same-modality and
cross-modality pairs do separate on these six measurements, and that
separation is what the script computes. The accuracy sentence is a
separate assertion that no computation produced.

Required action: withdraw the accuracy sentence from the contract
results table and the handoff, or demote it to a proposed question
with the design that would answer it, namely more detectors, validated
accuracy for every one, a pre-declared metric, and intervals that
respect both the pair-sharing and the instance clustering.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
