### 2. absence coerced to a confident miss
`cam.get(i, -1.0) < thr` cannot distinguish 'detector emitted nothing'
from 'detector emitted a low score'. Both become a confident miss.
  mapillary    absent   32,787 of 134,565 rows   =  24.4% of rows   =  52.5% of its misses

  For a detection task an unmatched ground-truth object is a genuine
  false negative, so this coercion is defensible. It is recorded because
  the charter forbids silent coercion, not because it is wrong here.

Finding. The point estimate is unchanged and the direction is intact:
the cluster-robust interval [1.144, 1.166] excludes 1.0, so the two
channels do fail together beyond what class, range and visibility explain.
Result E's substantive conclusion stands.

What does not stand is the size of the claimed evidence. The published
CMH of 4,924 is computed at the box unit and overstates the evidence
by a factor of about 5. The honest statistic is on the order of
982, and the correct denominator for any RSS-style evidence-count
argument is roughly 8,976 independent tracked objects, not 134,565 boxes.

Results D, G and H share the box-level unit and are affected the same way.
None of their point estimates change. Every interval and test statistic
derived from them must be restated at the instance unit before use.
