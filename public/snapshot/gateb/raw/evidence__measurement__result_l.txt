----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The conditional coefficient does not fall to independence. On a fixed
population with five admissible confounders it is 1.151, with a
cluster-robust 95% interval of [1.138, 1.160] that excludes 1.0.

Result E's open question is answered for the covariates available here:
shared observable difficulty explains most of the marginal association
and does not explain all of it. Camera and lidar fail together beyond
what class, range, visibility, weather and motion account for.

This is bounded by the covariates nuScenes annotates. Unmeasured common
causes remain possible and no adjustment set here is claimed to be
sufficient for identification. The result is association after declared
conditioning, not a causal effect.
