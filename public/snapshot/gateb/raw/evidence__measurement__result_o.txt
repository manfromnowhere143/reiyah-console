PROVENANCE
  tool     : tools/measure/result_o_sensitivity_evalue.py
  commands :
    python3 tools/measure/result_o_sensitivity_evalue.py \
        gt_val_cache.json matched_mapillary.json matched_megvii.json
    python3 tools/measure/result_o_sensitivity_evalue.py \
        gt_val_cache.json matched_mapillary.json matched_pointpillars.json

  question : Results L, M and N end on the same honest caveat - the coupling is measured
             after the covariates nuScenes annotates, and an UNMEASURED common cause of
             both failures could produce it. This quantifies that caveat instead of only
             declaring it: how strong would such a hidden factor have to be.

  instrument: E-value (VanderWeele and Ding, 2017). The minimum strength of association, on
             the risk-ratio scale, that an unmeasured common cause would need with BOTH
             camera failure and lidar failure, beyond the five measured confounders, to move
             the observed conditional association to independence. E(x) = x + sqrt(x(x-1)).

  estimator: Mantel-Haenszel conditional risk ratio of lidar-miss comparing camera-miss to
             camera-hit objects, computed DIRECTLY from the same 2x2 cells the coefficient
             uses (a=both miss, b=cam miss/lid hit, c=cam hit/lid miss, d=both hit):
               RR_MH = sum a(c+d)/n  /  sum c(a+b)/n     (Greenland-Robins)
             No odds-ratio-to-risk-ratio approximation is used, so the common-outcome caveat
             of the OR-based E-value does not apply here.

  self-check: the Mantel-Haenszel ODDS ratio at score>=0.30 is 2.776 (megvii), against
             Result E's independently derived conditional MH OR of 2.810 on the shallower
             class x range x visibility stratification. Adding weather and motion lowers it
             slightly, as expected, confirming the machinery reproduces the established
             conditional association.

FINDING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the score>=0.30 reference point, an unmeasured common cause of camera failure and lidar
failure would have to be associated with EACH, on the risk-ratio scale and beyond class,
range, visibility, weather and motion, by a factor of at least:

  mapillary x megvii         E-value 3.03   (2.88 for the near-null 95% bound)
  mapillary x pointpillars   E-value 2.13   (2.05 for the near-null 95% bound)

to explain the coupling away. A hidden factor weaker than that on either arm cannot account
for it; one at least that strong on both could. For scale, that is an unmeasured shared
difficulty comparable to the strongest MEASURED covariate effects in this data, not a slight
residual imbalance.

Stated as measured, not oversold:

  1. The E-value falls as the threshold tightens (megvii 4.73 -> 2.28; pointpillars 3.16 ->
     2.05), tracking the coupling's own attenuation in Result N. The strictest operating
     points ask the least of a confounder; the loosest ask the most.

  2. The E-value bounds plausibility; it does not test whether such a confounder exists.
     Object size and truncation are named unmeasured candidates. This result says how strong
     one would have to be, not that one is present or absent.

Three independent robustness axes now stand behind the finding:
  M  the detector          coupling survives a second, architecturally distinct lidar backbone
  N  the score threshold   conditional coefficient excludes 1.0 across the whole 0.1-0.5 range
  O  unmeasured confounding a hidden common cause must reach RR >= ~2-3 on both arms to nullify

NON-CLAIMS
  Association after declared conditioning, not a causal effect. The E-value bounds unmeasured
  confounding; it neither rules it in nor out. Bounded by the covariates nuScenes annotates.
  Two published detection outputs on one public split, retained as proposed. No released 1.2
  byte is modified.
